# Play Store listing draft

## App name
SlicePilot

## Short description
Slice STL and 3MF models for your next 3D print.

## Full description
SlicePilot is a little slicer for your next 3D print.

Load STL or 3MF models, choose printer settings, slice in the background, preview the result, and send the job to a supported Elegoo/YE3D printer.

Features:
- Open STL and 3MF files from your phone
- Background slicing with progress notifications
- G-code preview and print time/material estimates
- Cloud sign-in and printer selection
- Cute, simple mobile workflow
